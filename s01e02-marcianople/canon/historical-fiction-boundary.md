# Historical and fictional contract — S01E02

The production is a **clearly labeled fictionalized retelling** built around historical events.

## Historical anchors

- Gothic refugees sought admission across the Danube in **376 CE**.
- Roman authorities exploited, starved, humiliated, and failed to provision them.
- Ancient accounts describe desperate Goths trading children into slavery for food.
- Gothic leaders attended a banquet hosted by **Lupicinus** at **Marcianople**.
- Fighting erupted; **Fritigern** escaped and rejoined his people.
- Lupicinus marched against the Goths approximately **nine miles** from Marcianople.
- The Roman force was decisively defeated; Lupicinus survived and escaped.
- Many Goths recovered or acquired Roman weapons from defeated soldiers.

## Deliberate fictional inventions

- Fritigern is **Alaric's father**.
- Young Alaric and his **older brother** are present during the migration and Marcianople crisis.
- Alaric's older brother is **exchanged for a loaf of bread**.
- Fritigern knows the banquet is an execution trap **before** accepting.
- Banquet retinue conducts a coordinated counteroperation: guard killings, uniform exchange, armory infiltration, rock substitution, wagon extraction of confiscated weapons.
- A limited cache of personal weapons was **smuggled** through careless Roman searches.
- Fritigern defeats Lupicinus near the family wagons, sees Alaric watching, and **chooses mercy**.
- Alaric later carries a **damaged Roman helmet** from the battlefield as foreshadowing.
