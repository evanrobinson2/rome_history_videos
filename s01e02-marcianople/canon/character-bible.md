## Fritigern

- Early-to-mid 40s.
- Tall, extremely broad-shouldered; thick neck; large damaged hands.
- Long weathered trapezoidal face, broad cheekbones, blunt chin.
- Broken nose displaced slightly left; shallow scar through right eyebrow.
- Deep-set dark eyes beneath heavy horizontal brows.
- Wavy neck-length dark hair; thick beard in two rough practical braids.
- Charcoal-blue wool cloak, plain iron disc clasp; smoke-brown tunic; wide worn belt.
- At Marcianople battle: **banquet clothing, no armor**.
- Stillness, low center of gravity, economical violence. Never berserk or regal.

## Child Alaric (~5–7 fictional chronology)

- Small, wiry, underfed, alert.
- Narrow oval face; enormous wide-set gray-blue eyes.
- Thick dark brows with gap above left eye.
- Thick unruly dark curls; persistent center-forehead curl.
- Oversized charcoal-gray tunic; faded blue hooded wrap.
- Braided cord tied **twice around left wrist**.
- Shock = stillness and searching eyes, not theatrical screaming.

## Alaric's older brother (~11–12)

- Tall for age; long-limbed; narrow shoulders.
- Long youthful face; hazel-green eyes; uneven dark-brown hair at ears.
- Small notch upper left ear; one chipped front tooth.
- Gray-brown tunic; patched dark trousers; **faded rust-red scarf**.
- Braided cord on **right wrist**.
- During separation: looks back at family; understands before Alaric does.

## Alaric's mother / Fritigern's wife

- Early-to-mid 30s; tall, lean, physically capable.
- Long oval face; high cheekbones; strong straight nose; gray-blue eyes.
- Dark auburn-brown hair in one heavy braid.
- Fine scar below right cheekbone; weather-darkened skin.
- Deep ochre shawl; faded blue-gray dress; woven belt; **bronze horse-shaped clasp**.
- Long cracked fingers; split left thumbnail.
- Never passive Madonna or glamorous warrior queen.

## Roman soldiers

- Late 4th-c Eastern Roman: ridge helmets, oval shields, mail/scale, spears, spathae, muted red/gray tunics.
- Individually human, often very young — not faceless sci-fi troops.
- Terror from failed assumptions and collapsing command, not inherent cowardice.

## Gothic defenders

- Refugee population with trained fighting-age men, hunters, riders, archers — weapons as culture and survival.
- Worn wool, belts, wrapped forearms, travel cloaks, practical boots, mismatched recovered gear.
- **Hunger conceals capability; it does not create it.**

## Vehicles (explicit negatives)

Low squat four-wheeled timber carts; short sideboards; thick wheels; felt/wool/rawhide covers on low hoops; ox/mule/small horse draft.

**Never:** Conestoga, prairie wagon, tall white canvas bonnet, stagecoach, ornate carriage.
