# Dialogue — S01E02

## Fritigern — battle address (B03 / pre-contact)

> Children beneath the wagons. The wounded among the trees.  
> We crossed the river for land—not war. For bread. For peace.  
> But they come believing hunger has made us helpless.  
> If you can string a bow, string it.  
> If you can raise a shield, stand beside me.  
> If you can lift a sword—  
> let it fall upon the men who have come to murder your family.

Deliver without shouting at first. Final two lines rise only enough to reach the formation. No cheering — people exchange looks, fasten belts, and move.

## Mercy subtext (B15)

Do not voice Fritigern's full internal monologue. Image and eye line carry it.

Optional restrained narration if unclear:

> He had taken up the knife for his child. He would not teach the child to worship it.

Use only if the sequence needs it.
