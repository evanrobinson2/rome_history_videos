## Silhouette signature

**Shorter than Fritigern.** Narrower shoulders. **Cloak pinned at one shoulder**
creating an asymmetric **triangle drape** — the single fastest ID marker. **Full beard
point** extending below chin (deckle edge). Hands habitually at belt, not at sides.
No prominent sword silhouette.

## Distinguish from Fritigern

See `FRI-001.md` comparison table. At banquet: seated **beside** not opposite
Lupicinus; slightly turned toward Fritigern.

## Anti-caricature note

Thin does not mean weak or sneaky. Same paper weight and cut quality as Fritigern.
Beard is shape, not villain goatee.

## Banquet state (`ALA-001-BANQUET`)

Both hands visible on table edge. Cloak triangle drape falls to bench beside him.
Slightly withdrawn posture — smaller negative space around figure.
