## Silhouette signature

**Tallest principal.** Broad shoulders, long cloak with **fur deckle edge only at
shoulders** (jagged torn-paper rim). Hair mass to shoulders, **clean jaw line**
(little or no beard bulge). **Sword on left hip** — long vertical scabbard shape.
Belt pouch bulge on right hip. Stands **upright, head slightly forward** — listening
posture, not heroic chin-up.

## Anti-caricature note

Not a king's crown shape. Not sanctified (no centred halo, no gold on person).
Not the largest silhouette in every frame — in crowds he is found by **shoulder fur
deckle + height**, not spotlight.

## Banquet state (`FRI-001-BANQUET`)

Cloak open at front showing layered tunic. Seated on **bench** (not Roman chair).
Torso leaned slightly forward. Drinking horn or bowl silhouette on table — not Roman
cup shape.
